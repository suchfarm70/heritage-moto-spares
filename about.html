Engineering Heritage & Provenance

Preserving Two-Wheeled History Through Forensic Engineering

Born From Workshop Frustration in Karol Bagh & Grant Road

Founded by vintage motorcycle restorers and mechanical engineers, Heritage Moto Spares was created to solve an acute crisis in India’s motorcycling heritage. As factory assembly lines closed for iconic models—such as the Cast Iron Royal Enfield Bullet, Ideal Jawa Mysore 2-strokes, and Escorts Rajdoot 350s—dealership networks dropped support. Internet marketplaces became flooded with dangerously substandard counterfeit reproductions: soft aluminum pistons that seize under heat, poorly cast brass jets with incorrect flow rates, and mismatched metric bolts that strip irreplaceable vintage crankcases.

We set out to build a transparent, forensic parts verification infrastructure rooted in India's two historic motorcycling nerve centers: Karol Bagh (New Delhi) and Grant Road (Mumbai).

Our Three Pillars of Mechanical Integrity

1. Forensic Verification & Metallurgy Lab

Every single component admitted to our vaults undergoes strict inspection:

  • Thread Standard Inspection: Optical comparator checking for 26 TPI British Standard Cycle (CEI), 55° Whitworth (BSW/BSF), and Unified (UNF) standards to protect classic casings.
  • Hardness & Material Testing: Rockwell C testing on transmission gears (En-36B case-hardened alloy) and crankpins to prevent premature dog wear.
  • Carburetor Flow Testing: Genuine Amal, Pacco P47R, and Mikuni VM28 bodies tested on depression flow benches to ensure factory-calibrated fuel delivery.

2. Physical Vaults in Delhi & Mumbai

We operate real physical storage vaults with walk-in coordination for veteran mechanics and collectors:

  • Delhi NCR Vault (Karol Bagh & Mayapuri): Deep pipelines for heavy cast iron singles, Redditch transmission assemblies, and vintage British twins.
  • Mumbai MMR Hub (Grant Road & Kurla): Rapid-dispatch inventory of Czechoslovakian-spec two-stroke parts, Pacco carburetors, and coastal-grade stainless dampers.

3. Transparent Provenance Classification

We maintain strict honest disclosure: every part is clearly labeled as Factory Sealed NOS (New Old Stock), Flow-Tested Original OEM, or UK/EU Remanufactured Spec. Never dubious unbranded reproductions.
