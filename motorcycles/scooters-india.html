The Golden Era of Indian Two-Wheelers • 1960–2002

Authentic Spare Parts for Vintage Indian Scooters

For over four decades, classic two-stroke geared and automatic scooters formed the bedrock of middle-class mobility across India. From the ubiquitous metallic-green Bajaj Chetak 150 and high-revving 5-port LML Vespa NV, to the timeless elegance of the Lambretta Li150 / SIL Vijay Super and the electric-start ease of the Kinetic Honda DX, Heritage Moto Spares maintains dedicated parts vaults in Karol Bagh (Delhi) and Grant Road (Mumbai) to keep these cultural legends on the road.

Technical Engineering Signatures by Platform

  • • Bajaj Chetak & Super 150 (Akurdi Tooling): Horizontal rotary valve intake, Spaco SI 20/20D box carburetor, case-hardened 4-speed cruciform sliding selector cross, and monocoque pressed-steel cowl chassis.
  • • LML Vespa 150 NV & Select (Piaggio Italy License): Advanced 5-port scavenge transfer barrel, flat-profile cruciform cross, 12V 80W 5-wire electronic CDI magneto, and electric start ring gear.
  • • Lambretta Li150 & SIL Vijay Super (Innocenti Milan Lineage): Tubular backbone steel frame with removable side cowls, Mahle-spec 150cc barrel, Dell'Orto SH2/22 carburetor, and direct duplex primary chain with bevel final drive.
  • • Kinetic Honda DX (Honda Motor Co. Japan Collaboration): 97.8cc horizontal single, centrifugal dry clutch with Bando Kevlar V-belt CVT variator, Mitsuba 12V starter motor, and automatic wax-pellet Keihin thermo-choke.
