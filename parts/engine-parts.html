Classic Motorcycle Engine Parts: Rebuild & Restoration Spares

Precision components for vintage single, twin, and multi-cylinder internal combustion engines.

Sourcing Obsolete Vintage Engine Components

The engine is the mechanical heart of any historic motorcycle restoration. Over decades of operation, internal components suffer from thermal stress, bore ovality, and journal wear. Finding factory-correct replacements for pre-unit British singles, 2-stroke Indian roadsters, or early Japanese four-cylinder engines is one of the highest-friction challenges in motorcycle preservation.

Primary Engine Component Assemblies Supported

  • Pistons & Ring Sets: Standard (STD) and oversize (+0.020", +0.040", +0.060") forged and cast pistons with correct ring-gap tolerances for Royal Enfield Cast Iron 350, Jawa 250 Type 353, and Norton Commando 750/850.
  • Valves, Springs & Guides: Stellite-faced exhaust valves, intake valves, phosphor-bronze valve guides, and dual progressive valve spring sets.
  • Oil Pump Assemblies: Precision worm drives, feed and return spindle sets, and check-valve balls for Royal Enfield pre-unit engines (OEM # 110056).
  • Crankshaft & Bearings: Conrod kits, big-end needle roller bearings, drive-side and timing-side main bearings.
  • Gasket Sets: Period-correct copper head gaskets, oil-resistant fiber crankcase gaskets, and Viton pushrod tube O-rings.

Need Fitment Verification?

Before ordering, verify your engine serial number stamped on the left crankcase web. If your component is out of production, submit a Rare Part Sourcing Request for expert inventory checking.
